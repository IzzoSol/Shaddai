The wind whispers secrets in my ear, 
Of far-off lands and memories so dear. 
It carries scents of blooming flowers, 
And the soft rustle of autumn's final hours. 
 
The sun sets slow, a fiery ball, 
Painting the sky with hues of all. 
The stars appear, like diamonds bright, 
Twinkling and winking, in the evening's delight. 
 
The world is full of wonder and might, 
A tapestry of moments, day and night. 
The wind whispers truths, of love and strife, 
And the beauty that's found, in the midst of life. 
 
So let the wind whisper its secrets to me, 
And let my heart be filled, with wonder and glee. 
For in its gentle breeze, I find my peace, 
And a sense of belonging, to the world's release.
