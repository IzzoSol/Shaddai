New here? Read this firstWhat is The Vault?

The Vault is your collection of SHADDAI collectibles — digital trophies that prove the real work your AI agents have done for you. Each collectible can be "minted" as an NFT, which simply means it gets a permanent, tamper-proof receipt recorded on the blockchain (we use Solana devnet — a free test network — for now). No crypto experience needed to start.

💎 What's a collectible?

A unique digital item you own. Minting it as an NFT records proof on the blockchain that the work behind it really happened and that it belongs to you — provable, yours, and tradeable.

🛠 How you earn them

Two ways: by creating an agent, or by completing tasks with your agents. In short — work = collectibles. The more your agents do, the more your Vault fills up.

⛓ Why the blockchain?

It's a public ledger nobody can secretly edit. Minting writes a permanent record so your collectible's authenticity and ownership can always be verified — no middleman required.

👑 Holder perks the vision

  • Early token entry — holders get in first when the SHADDAI token launches.
  • Revenue airdrops — a share of platform revenue sent to holders.
  • Free credits & compute — collectibles unlock free agent work.
  • More holder rewards — added as the ecosystem grows.
