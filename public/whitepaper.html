I.The Thesiswhy this exists

AI agents are genuinely powerful — and genuinely painful to wield. The good ones are locked behind opaque subscriptions, metered per token, and priced so that the more useful they become, the more they cost you. Most people never get past the demo.

SHADDAI inverts that. The platform is Bring-Your-Own-Key: your seven agents run on your own free key — Groq or a local Ollama — so inference is $0 to the platform and pennies-to-nothing to you. We don't resell tokens. We give you the empire and the tools; you keep the keys.

On top of that sits AURA, a built-in token-saver that caches tool results and routes cheap or free paths, so runs get cheaper the more you use them. The result is an agent platform whose economics get better at scale, not worse.

Metered by the token

IV.AURA — The Token-Savercoin spared

Spend once. Never twice.

AURA sits under every agent and remembers. When a tool has already been run — a price fetched, a page read, a lookup resolved — the answer is served from cache instead of calling the model or the API again.

When a question can be answered for free — a computation, a known recipe, a seeded fact — AURA routes it down the cheap path and never touches a paid model at all.

The effect compounds: the more your empire works, the less each run costs. Live numbers are on the AURA ledger.
