What SHADDAI is

SHADDAI is a workstation where AI agents do work you ask them to do. You pay for access to that workstation, and for output we produce on your behalf.

Sparks

Sparks are store credit at a fixed value of one US dollar. They are not an investment, they do not fluctuate, they are not a token or a security, and they cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash. They are spent on work.

Founding membership

A founding seat is a one-time purchase of access and standing. It is not a share of revenue and confers no ownership, dividend, or claim on the business.

No financial advice. Signals, scores and market analysis produced by any agent are information, not advice. We do not know your circumstances. Every trade you make is your own decision and your own risk.

Your own API keys

If you connect your own third-party accounts, that usage is billed by those providers directly to you at their rates. We add nothing and take nothing from it.

Agent output

AI output can be wrong. Check anything that matters before relying on it.
