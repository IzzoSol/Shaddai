# Type Strategies, Signatures & Not-Self Themes

Each Human Design Type has a **Strategy** (the correct way to engage with life), a **Signature** (the feeling that confirms you are living correctly), and a **Not-Self theme** (the feeling that signals you are off-track, usually from forcing or resisting your Strategy).

| Type | Strategy | Signature | Not-Self Theme |
|------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **Manifestor** | Inform before acting | Peace | Anger |
| **Generator** | Wait to respond | Satisfaction | Frustration |
| **Manifesting Generator** | Wait to respond, then inform | Satisfaction (& peace) | Frustration & anger |
| **Projector** | Wait for the invitation | Success | Bitterness |
| **Reflector** | Wait a lunar cycle (~28 days) | Surprise / delight | Disappointment |

## Manifestor — Inform before acting

Manifestors carry initiating energy and are the only Type built to act without waiting for an external cue. Their friction comes from acting in isolation and triggering resistance in others. The **Strategy of informing** — telling affected people *before* they act — removes that resistance. Living correctly brings **peace**; the not-self signal is **anger** (their own and the anger they provoke).

## Generator — Wait to respond

Generators have a defined Sacral center, the engine of sustainable life-force energy. They are not designed to initiate from the mind; instead they **respond** to what life brings — people, opportunities, questions. A genuine response is felt in the body as a gut "uh-huh" (yes) or "unh-unh" (no). Responding to work they love yields **satisfaction**; ignoring the gut and pushing into the wrong things yields **frustration**.

## Manifesting Generator — Respond, then inform

A hybrid: Sacral response energy plus a motor connected to the Throat, giving them speed and the ability to manifest. They are multi-passionate, skip steps, and move fast. Strategy is to **respond first** (like a Generator) and then **inform** (like a Manifestor) before they leap, so others can keep up. Their signature is **satisfaction** layered with **peace**; the not-self is **frustration and anger**.

## Projector — Wait for the invitation

Projectors have no defined Sacral and are not consistent energy types; their genius is **seeing and guiding** other people's energy. They thrive when their gifts are recognized and they are formally **invited** into the big things (relationships, work, places to live). Correct invitation brings **success** and recognition; forcing their way in, unrecognized, brings **bitterness** and exhaustion.

## Reflector — Wait a lunar cycle

Reflectors have all nine centers undefined; they are deeply open and sample the energy of everyone around them. Because their experience shifts constantly, big decisions should ripen over a full **lunar cycle (~28 days)** rather than be made on the spot. Living correctly brings **surprise and delight** at life; being rushed or in an unhealthy environment brings **disappointment**.

## Practical note for collaboration

Strategy describes *how each Type should engage a task or each other*: Manifestors and Manifesting Generators inform; Generators and MGs respond; Projectors wait for the invitation to lead or advise; Reflectors take time before committing. Respecting these patterns reduces friction in a team — see agent-collaboration.md for the SHADDAI mapping.
