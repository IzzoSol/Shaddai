This Refund Policy explains when you can and cannot get a refund on SHADDAI. It is part of our Terms of Service. Everything on SHADDAI is a digital good. Please read this before you buy — it is shown before purchase.

Short version: Subscriptions can be canceled anytime and you keep access until the end of the period. Digital items and Sparks that have already been delivered are generally non-refundable. If the law where you live gives you stronger rights (like EU/UK statutory rights), we honor those.

1. Subscriptions

  • You can cancel anytime from your account. Cancellation stops the next renewal.
  • When you cancel, your access continues until the end of the current paid period — you are not cut off immediately.
  • We generally do not provide partial or pro-rated refunds for the unused part of a billing period, except where required by law.
  • If you were charged due to a clear billing error on our side (for example, double-charged), contact us and we will make it right.

2. Sparks (No Cash Value, Non-Refundable)

"Sparks" are a soft in-app currency with no cash value and are not redeemable for money. Sparks are earned in the Service or included with a subscription — they are not sold for cash on their own, and there is no cash-out. Once Sparks are delivered to your account, they are non-refundable, and unused Sparks are not exchangeable for money. See the full definition in our Terms.

3. Collectibles and Items

  • Items and Collectibles are digital goods delivered instantly. Once delivered, they are generally non-refundable.
  • Items are sold with contents disclosed up front at a fixed price — you know what you are getting before you buy.
  • Items are not investments and we make no promise they will hold or gain value; a change in perceived value is not grounds for a refund.
  • Randomized rewards are only ever obtained with earned Sparks, never a direct real-money purchase, so there is no cash purchase to refund.

4. Your Legal Rights (EU / UK and Others)

Nothing in this policy removes rights you have under mandatory consumer law. In particular:

  • If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you may have a statutory right to cancel certain purchases within a set period. However, for digital content, that right can end once delivery/performance begins with your consent. By purchasing and choosing immediate access to a digital item or Sparks, you agree that delivery begins immediately and acknowledge you may lose the cancellation right once delivery has started.
  • Where the law still requires a refund despite the above, or where a product is faulty or not as described, we will honor your statutory refund rights.
