II · How it works

From your context to published work

FLOW 4 steps

No jargon. You bring the context, a council of specialists does the work, you approve it, and it ships. Simple enough to trust, deep enough to scale.

◈
Step 01

Your context

Feed in your goal, your notes, your data. The system reads it and figures out what needs doing.

✦
Step 02

The council of specialists

Seven agents — research, writing, markets, security and more — collaborate, each doing the part it's best at.

☑
Step 03

An approval step

Nothing goes out blind. You review, tweak, and give the green light — you stay in control.

▲
Step 04

Published output

Finished work — content, code, analysis — delivered where you need it. AURA keeps the cost low along the way.
