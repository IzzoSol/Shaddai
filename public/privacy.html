What we store

Your account (email or sign-in identity), what you asked agents to do, your Sparks balance and transactions, and preferences such as your chosen theme and filters.

Your API keys

Keys you add to the vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password only you know. That password is never stored. We cannot read your keys. This is arithmetic, not a policy — without your password the stored bytes decrypt to nothing. If you forget it, the keys are unrecoverable and you paste them again.

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your prompts to train models. We do not share your agent output with other users.

Third parties

Payments run through Stripe, which handles card details — we never see them. Market data comes from public sources such as DexScreener. If you connect your own provider accounts, those providers see the requests your agents make.

Local storage

Your browser keeps your theme, filters and sign-in token. Clearing site data removes them.

Deletion

Ask and we will delete your account and its data.
