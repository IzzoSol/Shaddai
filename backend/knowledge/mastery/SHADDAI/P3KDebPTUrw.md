## THESIS
The development and design of products, particularly Codex, require a high-quality bar and a deep understanding of "taste," which involves aesthetic, systems thinking, and cultural aspects. The role of humans in building products is shifting from writing code to steering AI, and it's essential to be open to learning, growth, and change. The goal is to create a seamless and extensible platform that combines the strengths of Codex and ChatGPT.

## FRAMEWORKS & PRINCIPLES
- Emphasize "taste" in product development, considering aesthetic, systems thinking, and cultural aspects
- Prioritize building a high-quality product with a high bar for quality
- Leverage AI for automation and steering, rather than traditional coding
- Foster a culture of experimentation, learning, and growth
- Encourage autonomy and agency in personal and professional development
- Focus on finding product market fit, both for products and individual roles

### The Role of Prototypes and Documents
- Key teaching: Prototypes and documents are still useful, but it's essential to pick the right medium for the point being made, and prototypes can sometimes be the wrong first thing to do.
- Quote: "If implementation is abundant, then it's really important to pick the right format for the point you're trying to make."

### Understanding "Taste"
- Key teaching: "Taste" refers to the ability to make decisions about what to work on, how to present information, and what medium to use, and it involves a combination of aesthetic, systems thinking, and cultural aspects.
- Quote: "There is an aesthetic part to it, but there's also a systems thinking part of it, like how does this fit in the system?"

- Quote: "I think design's a little bit harder to grade because the human aspect of taste is like part of the feedback mechanism you need."

### Human Brains and Creativity
- Key teaching: Human brains continue to be valuable for creativity and coming up with new ideas, and AI is better at patterns and existing paradigms.
- Quote: "Let's give it up for the human brain for now."

### The Design Process
- Key teaching: The traditional design process is dead, and design is now more about steering things as they move along, with a focus on building and iterating quickly.
- Quote: "I agree with her take that it is dead, and I genuinely was not a fan of this process before AI."

### The Evolution of Design Process
- Key teaching: The traditional design process is being disrupted by the speed of implementation and the availability of new tools, requiring a re-evaluation of the role of design and the skills required to be successful.
- Quote: "I think that's what's kind of starting to erode, is that sort of gatekeeping that are like, well, no, this like being good at this role is being good at this tool."

- Key teaching: The concept of "zone defense" in product work refers to the idea of spreading out and covering different areas, rather than having a single person or team responsible for a specific area.
