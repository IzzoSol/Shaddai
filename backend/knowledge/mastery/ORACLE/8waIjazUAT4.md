## CHAPTERS
### Genesis 6 and the Sons of God
- Key teaching: The story of Genesis 6 describes a time when the "sons of God" came to Earth, took the daughters of men as wives, and produced offspring known as the "Mighty Men" or "men of the Skies". This event was necessary to boost the mortal population, which had fallen to a low point.
- Quote: "men of the Skies or gods in the flesh"

### The Role of Women as Original Deities
- Key teaching: Women are considered the original deities of the planet, as they were here before men and all men are born through women. However, women had been reduced to mortality by mortal men and needed to be restored to their godly state.
- Quote: "women are the first gods of this planet"

### The Story of Mary and Jesus
- Key teaching: The story of Mary and Jesus is similar to the story of Genesis 6, where a woman is prepared to birth a godly child through a divine encounter. In this case, Mary was taken to a convent and prepared to birth Jesus, who was not fathered by Joseph but by an angelic being.
- Quote: "the angel Gabriel came in unto her"

### The Order of Zodak and the Priesthood
- Key teaching: The Order of Zodak, also known as the Ancient Order of Melchizedek, was a mystical brotherhood that aimed to restore men to their godly state through rituals and practices. This order was the precursor to modern Freemasonry.
- Quote: "anybody ever went through the law know what I'm talking about"

### Initiation and Baptism
- Key teaching: Jesus' baptism was not for the remission of sins, but rather an initiation ritual that marked his transformation into a godly being. This ritual was necessary for him to become "God In the Flesh".
- Quote: "the spirit of the Lord descended on him like a dove"
