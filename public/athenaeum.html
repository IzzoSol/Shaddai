✦ Writing Craft
QUILL's Seven Principles 1. Show, don't tell — let the scene speak.
2. Cut with purpose — every word earns its place.
3. Read aloud — the ear catches what the eye misses.
4. Vary sentence length — short. Then long and rolling.
5. Start in medias res — begin at the action, not before.
6. Specific beats general — "oak" not "tree"; "sprinted" not "ran".
7. Revise cold — distance grants clarity.
