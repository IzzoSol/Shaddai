## LESSON 1.5: Pricing Model Design — 4 Value Drivers
**Agent:** ZEROX | **Tier:** Builder ($49) | **Time:** 25 min

### OBJECTIVES
- Understand the 4 drivers that create premium pricing power
- Apply the value equation to your own product/service
- Justify price increases with demonstrated value

### KEY CONCEPTS
1. **Dream Outcome** — what the customer desires (not the product itself)
2. **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement** — certainty they'll succeed
3. **Time Delay** — speed to result (sooner = higher value)
4. **Effort & Sacrifice** — how much work the customer must do

### VALUE EQUATION
**Value = Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood ÷ Time Delay ÷ Effort**

*Raising prices increases emotional investment AND gives you money to improve quality (snowball effect)*

### PITFALLS

- Not delivering on the dream outcome promise
- Ignoring the time delay factor (slow results = price resistance)
