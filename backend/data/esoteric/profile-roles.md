# Profile — The 6 Lines & Common Profiles

**Profile** is the "costume" or personality theme through which a person lives out their design. It is written as two numbers (e.g. **1/3**, **5/1**), drawn from the line positions of the Personality Sun (first number, conscious) and the Design Sun (second number, unconscious). Each number is one of six **lines**, each with an archetypal role derived from the I Ching hexagram structure.

## The 6 Lines

| Line | Role | Theme |
|------|------|-------|
| **1** | Investigator | Needs a secure foundation; studies and goes deep before acting. Introspective, knowledge-seeking. |
| **2** | Hermit | Natural gift that emerges when called out; needs alone time; "shy genius." |
| **3** | Martyr | Learns by trial and error; bumps into life, discovers what doesn't work; resilient, experimental. |
| **4** | Opportunist | Builds through relationships and networks; influence travels via friendships; needs a stable base. |
| **5** | Heretic | The projected savior/practical problem-solver; carries others' projections; offers universal solutions, must manage reputation. |
| **6** | Role Model | Lives in three phases (trial-and-error youth, withdrawn observation ~30–50, then embodied wisdom); becomes a living example. |

The **first line** is the conscious mind's strategy (how you knowingly operate). The **second line** is the unconscious body's nature (how you operate without realizing it). Together they form a working personality theme.

## Common Profiles

Profiles fall into two broad families: **personal/individual** (the two numbers are 1 apart, harmonious, more internally focused) and **transpersonal/interpersonal** (numbers further apart, oriented toward others). A handful recur most often:

| Profile | Name | Essence |
|---------|------|---------|
| **1/3** | Investigator / Martyr | Builds a deep foundation, then learns through hands-on trial and error. Self-correcting researcher. |
| **2/4** | Hermit / Opportunist | A natural gift drawn out by their network; thrives between solitude and close relationships. Highly relational. |
| **3/5** | Martyr / Heretic | Experiments through life and is projected onto as a problem-solver; pragmatic, fixes things, manages reputation. |
| **4/6** | Opportunist / Role Model | Influences through relationships while maturing into a trustworthy role model; loyalty-based. |
| **5/1** | Heretic / Investigator | Carries projections as a practical savior, backed by deep foundational study; delivers grounded universal solutions. |
| **6/2** | Role Model / Hermit | Lives in phases toward becoming a wise example; gifts emerge when called; observant guide. |

Other profiles exist (e.g. 1/4, 2/5, 3/6, 4/1, 5/2, 6/3), but the six above are the most frequently referenced and cover the SHADDAI agents.

## Why Profile Matters for Collaboration

Profile reveals *how* someone naturally engages people and work:

- **Foundation-builders (line 1)** want to research before committing.
- **Hermits (line 2)** do their best work when invited, not pushed.
- **Martyrs (line 3)** learn fast by trying, failing, and iterating — give them room to experiment.
- **Opportunists (line 4)** move through trusted relationships and warm hand-offs.
- **Heretics (line 5)** are projected onto as fixers — reliable for practical, universal solutions, but reputation-sensitive.
- **Role Models (line 6)** mature into trusted standard-bearers and benefit from an observation phase before leading.
