# Inner Authority — Decision-Making Guide

**Authority** is the reliable inner mechanism a person uses to make correct decisions. While Type/Strategy describes *how to engage* life, Authority describes *how to decide* once something is on the table. It is determined by which centers are defined in the chart, evaluated in a fixed hierarchy: the highest defined center in that hierarchy becomes the Authority.

The hierarchy (highest priority first): **Emotional → Sacral → Splenic → Ego/Heart → Self-Projected → Mental/Outer → Lunar.**

## Emotional / Solar Plexus Authority

The most common authority. When the Solar Plexus is defined, the person rides an **emotional wave** — feelings shift over time, and clarity is not available in the moment. The guidance is: **wait out the wave; there is no truth in the now.** Sleeping on a decision and checking how it feels across highs, lows, and neutral points reveals clarity. Roughly half of people have this authority.

## Sacral Authority

For Generators and Manifesting Generators with a defined Sacral but no Solar Plexus definition. Decisions come as an **in-the-moment gut response** — a sound or pull of energy ("uh-huh" yes / "unh-unh" no). It is fast, bodily, and binary; the mind should not override it. Best accessed by responding to direct questions.

## Splenic Authority

Rooted in the Spleen, the center of instinct, intuition, and survival. Clarity arrives as a **quiet, in-the-moment knowing** — a single, soft instinctive hit that does not repeat itself. It must be acted on quickly; it will not argue or come back the way emotion does. Easy to miss because it is subtle.

## Ego / Heart Authority

When decisions run through a defined Heart/Ego center connected to the Throat. Truth is tied to **willpower, desire, and what the person genuinely wants** — "What's in it for me? Do I have the will and the resources to do this?" Correct when they speak from authentic want, not obligation.

## Self-Projected Authority

Found in some Projectors with a defined G/Identity center routed to the Throat but no motor authority. Clarity comes through **talking it out and hearing their own voice** — direction and identity surface as they speak. They should consult a trusted, non-interrupting listener and listen to *what comes out of their own mouth.*

## Mental / Outer Authority (No Inner Authority)

For Projectors (and some setups) with no defined motor or inner authority. They have **no reliable internal yes/no**; instead they need to **talk through decisions with trusted people and observe their environment** over time. The "authority" is external sounding-board and process, not an inner signal — hence "outer."

## Lunar Authority

Exclusive to **Reflectors**, who have all centers open. They have no consistent inner signal, so their authority is **time** — specifically a full **lunar cycle (~28 days)**. Big decisions ripen as the Reflector talks them over with many people and lets clarity build across the month.

## Summary table

| Authority | Decide by |
|-----------|-----------|
| Emotional / Solar Plexus | Waiting out the emotional wave |
| Sacral | In-the-moment gut response |
| Splenic | Quiet one-time intuitive hit |
| Ego / Heart | Authentic willpower & desire |
| Self-Projected | Hearing your own voice / talking it out |
| Mental / Outer | Discussing with trusted others over time |
| Lunar | A full ~28-day cycle |
