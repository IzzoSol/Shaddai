    Straight Answers

    Do free referrals count?

    Yes — free signups earn you Sparks immediately. USDC commissions activate when your referral upgrades to a paid plan.

    How long does tracking last?

    Forever. If someone signs up free via your link and upgrades two years later, you still earn the commission.

    Can I refer myself?

    No — self-referrals are blocked. The system detects same-device, same-email, and account ownership overlap.

    Does SHADDAI have a tier above L3?

    Three tiers is the structure. SHADDAI's 25/10/5 already beats every major competitor. Depth compounds — a good L2 network earns more than direct referrals alone.

    What counts as a qualifying subscription?

    Pro ($30/mo) and Founder ($100 one-time) both qualify. Founder is counted at $30 equivalent for commission purposes.

    How do I use my Sparks?

    Log into the dashboard and open the 2D world (SHADDAI City). Sparks appear in your HUD and can be spent at markets, Siege entry, plot upgrades, and the forge.
