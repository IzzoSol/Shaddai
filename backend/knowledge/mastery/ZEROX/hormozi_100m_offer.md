# MASTERY CARD — "$100M Offers: The Value Equation" (Alex Hormozi)
Agent: ZEROX · Source: hormozi_100m_offer · Type: business/offer-construction

## THESIS
Value is engineered, not guessed. By systematically maximizing a value equation and
de-constraining every step of the customer's journey, you can 2–3x both your PRICE and
your CLOSE RATE at the same time — producing stepwise jumps in company value (e.g. $2M → $10M
in a year "changing nothing but the core offer").

## CORE FRAMEWORK — The Value Equation
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
Raise the top, shrink the bottom. Four levers:

1. DREAM OUTCOME — decides whether someone cares about your CATEGORY at all. Tied to status
   (broadly: men → make money, women → look better). B2B commands higher prices than B2C
   because it is more closely tied to ROI. A $5 weight-loss PDF and a $50k liposuction solve
   the same desire — the other three levers explain the price gap.
2. PERCEIVED LIKELIHOOD OF ACHIEVEMENT — proof and track record. Same procedure, but the
   surgeon with 10,000 successful surgeries out-prices the fresh grad because certainty is
   higher. The opposite of likelihood is RISK — lower it with guarantees.
3. TIME DELAY — gap between buying and getting the result. Shorter = more valuable.
   Liposuction (sleep → wake up thinner) beats personal training (six-pack in 12–18 months).
4. EFFORT & SACRIFICE — two sides of one coin. EFFORT = new things they must START doing that
   they hate (wake up early, be sore). SACRIFICE = things they love they must STOP (Taco
   Tuesday, sleeping in).

## THE METHOD — Build a Grand Slam Offer
1. Itemize EVERY little step immediately before and after the purchase (e.g. weight loss also
   means: shop differently, learn to meal-prep).
2. For each step ask: What increases their risk? What makes it take longer? What makes them
   start doing things they hate? What makes them stop doing things they love?
3. Create a solution/bonus for each constraint → a "bonus stack."
4. Result: a more valuable AND higher-converting offer → double or triple price, close at a
   higher percentage.

## ENHANCERS (bolt-ons)
- SCARCITY — limit number of units.
- URGENCY — limit time.
- GUARANTEES — reverse risk. Four types: (1) unconditional ("money back if you ask"),
  (2) conditional ("guaranteed if you do X, Y, Z"), (3) anti-guarantee (lean into having none:
  "if you need a guarantee, this isn't for you"), (4) performance/implied ("if you don't make
  money, I don't make money").
- BONUSES — drive short-term buying decisions.

## SALES APPLICATION (hand-to-hand)
- Don't dump all seven bonuses up front. Make the ask on the initial offer.
- If they say no → diagnose the CONSTRAINT → plug in the matching bonus. Maybe stack three to
  get them over the edge.
- This lets the team STOP discounting: add value instead of cutting price.
- Post-purchase SURPRISE & DELIGHT with the remaining bonuses. Fast buyers who didn't need them
  get them free and love you; buyers who needed all seven got them on the call. Higher close
  rates, no discounts, consistent ops.
