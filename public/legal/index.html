Plain-English Summary

This summary is a quick guide, not a replacement for the full policies above. If anything conflicts, the full documents win.

  • SHADDAI is an early beta, run by one person, provided "as is." Things may change or break.
  • You must be 16 or older to use it.
  • Sparks are an in-app currency with no cash value. They are a limited, revocable license — not money, not property, not an investment. You cannot cash them out. Real money only flows in (to buy access/unlocks); there's no withdrawal.
  • Items and Collectibles are cosmetic/utility only. Their contents and price are shown before you buy. They are not investments and we don't promise they'll gain value. Any random rewards use earned Sparks — never a direct cash purchase.
  • Subscriptions (Free / Builder $9.99 / Pro $29.99 / Creator $79) are billed via Stripe. Cancel anytime; you keep access until the period ends.
  • Refunds: already-delivered digital goods and Sparks are generally non-refundable — except where the law (like EU/UK statutory rights) says otherwise. We honor those.
  • Privacy: we collect your account info and chat/content, use Stripe for payments (we don't store your card), and use cookies/localStorage to keep you signed in. You can request your data be deleted.
  • Chat is user-generated content — messages in shared spaces are visible to others. Don't post sensitive info.
  • Impact: a fixed percentage of platform fees is reserved for verified reforestation/impact efforts.

SHADDAI — beta. These policies are good-faith beta drafts, not filed legal advice. Have a lawyer review before scaling or before enabling any crypto or cash-out features.
