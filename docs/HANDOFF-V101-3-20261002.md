## 3. SPARKS AND PLOTS — what exists, and a proposal to settle it

### What exists in code today
**Sparks** (`backend/economy-service.js`, `economy-schema.sql`, `economy-routes.js`): a closed-loop soft-currency ledger. Append-only transactions, integer balances, idempotency keys, row-locked balance updates, and **no cash-out path by design**. Endpoints: balance, inventory, catalog, catalog/purchase, grant, spend. Related UI: Economy tab (burn mechanics, Bazaar, plot auctions); auction code sends 2% of a winning bid to a burn pool and 1% to a staking pool.

**Plots exist as three separate systems:**
1. `backend/lib/portal-plots-routes.js` — JSON file, rent/buy *intent*, "no real payment processing".
2. `backend/routes/eco-routes.js` + `eco-plots` — districts, plots priced in **USDC** (cap 5000), rent accrual in USDC.
3. `backend/deed-routes.js` — Metaplex Core NFT deeds on **devnet**, tiers RAW / DEVELOPED / LANDMARK.
UI: "Founders Row — 30 plots". Money units differ per system (Sparks, USDC, SOL), and nothing ties them together.

### Problems this causes
- Three plot registries can disagree about who owns what.
- USDC-priced plots plus accrued rent plus NFT deeds plus a staking pool reads as an investment product. Your own Founder Pass notes flag securities, gambling and MLM risk; the closed-loop Sparks decision says no yield.
- Sparks has real safeguards; plots and deeds don't share them.

### Proposal (a recommendation for you to decide on, not a legal opinion)
1. **Sparks = closed-loop only.** Earned by activity, spent on in-world goods and plot upgrades, **no yield, no staking, no cash-out**. Keep the ledger as is. Removing the staking pool UI was step one; next remove the backend credit calls in `auction-routes.js` (leave the 2% burn).
2. **One plot registry.** Pick one source of truth (the `eco-plots` model, since it has districts and caps) and make the portal and deed routes read from it. Retire the JSON-file portal system.
3. **Plots are a license to use space, priced in one unit.** Either Sparks (cleanest, stays closed-loop) or fixed USD through Stripe. Do not mix SOL/USDC/Sparks in one flow. Rent that "accrues" to the owner should be removed; owner income is what turns this into an investment product.
4. **Deeds/NFTs later, as a cosmetic receipt**, minted after a purchase, no resale promise, and only after a lawyer reviews it (per the Founder Pass draft).
5. **Scale plan:** run the ledger on Postgres (schema already written), keep idempotency keys on every grant/spend, per-user rate limits (exists: `moneyMoveLimit`), caps per account per day, a nightly reconciliation job (sum of ledger = sum of balances) and an alert when they diverge. Sparks sinks and sources should be a small, reviewed table, not scattered across routes.
6. **Before launch:** get legal review on: Sparks sold for USD (stored-value rules), plot sales, any resale or auction, and referral rewards.

### Decisions needed from you
- One plot currency: Sparks or USD?
- Remove accrued rent and the staking credit in the backend?
- Keep the NFT deed as a cosmetic receipt, or drop it for v1?
- Are Sparks ever sold for USD? (If yes, legal review before anything else.)

## 4. HOW TO OPEN IT LOCALLY WITHOUT SIGNING IN
Use the launcher on your Desktop: `Open SHADDAI (no login).bat` (see the launcher notes below). It does not change any app code.
