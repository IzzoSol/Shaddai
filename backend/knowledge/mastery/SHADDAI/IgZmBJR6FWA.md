### Historical Context of Transfer Pricing Regulations
- Key teaching: Early regulations emerged during World War I to prevent tax evasion and protect wartime revenues. The arm's length principle became a key pillar for international taxation rules.

### Arm's Length Principle in Transfer Pricing
- Key teaching: Multinational companies must price transactions between subsidiaries comparably with third parties, ensuring fair taxes across jurisdictions. This principle is codified globally and forms the basis for transfer pricing rules.
- Quote: "One solution...is that when one entity sells or transfers goods to another within a multinational company..."

### Complexity of Transfer Pricing
- Key teaching: Calculating accurate intercompany prices involves tangible assets like bottles, ingredients as well intangible ones such as trademarks. It's an intricate process likened more to art than science.
- Quote: "It's complicated...to price the intangible goods..." 

### Global Economy and Intangible Assets in Tax Disputes
- Key teaching: As economies become increasingly based on intangibles (like copyrights, patents), transfer pricing disputes have intensified due to their complexity. Companies must document intercompany transactions meticulously for tax authorities worldwide.
- Quote: "Transfer pricing disputes...have really heated up as the global economy has..."
