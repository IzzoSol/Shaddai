Straight Answers

What's "bring your own key"?

Plug in a free API key (Groq, Ollama and more). Your agents run on it, so inference is near-zero cost — you pay $30 for the platform, not per token.

What's a "receipt"?

Every run returns a signed record — which agent ran, which tools fired, what came back. Proof of work you can trust and re-check.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in one click. No lock-in, no contract. Your forged agents and office stay with your account.

What does Pro unlock?

Everything: all seven agents, the Forge, workflow chains, the SHADDAI MCP, premium brains, image generation, the 3D world and your office — one flat $30/mo.

What is SHADDAI MCP?

Your paid plan turns the whole Council into a Model Context Protocol server. From Claude, Cursor or any MCP client you can call the agents, fire their 300+ tools, and read their receipts — on your own key.

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?

No. Seven persona-tuned agents route across models, fire 300+ real tools and a 3,000-skill library, chain into multi-agent workflows, and log a receipt every run. Try it above — that's not a chat box.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Pick a job, hit run, approve the result. If you can send an email, you can run the Council.
