## THESIS
The development and application of AI technology require a unique blend of first-principles thinking, adaptability, and human judgment, with a focus on user value, experimentation, and collaboration. As AI continues to grow exponentially, it's essential to prioritize these principles to drive innovation and success. By embracing this approach, individuals and organizations can unlock the full potential of AI and create meaningful impact.

## FRAMEWORKS & PRINCIPLES
- First-principles thinking: understanding the fundamental principles of a problem and solving it from scratch
- Adaptability: being open to change and willing to pivot in response to new information or circumstances
- Human judgment: recognizing the importance of human nuance, experience, and decision-making in areas like product leadership and AI development
- User value: prioritizing the needs and experiences of users in AI development and application
- Experimentation: encouraging a culture of testing, learning, and iteration in AI development and application
