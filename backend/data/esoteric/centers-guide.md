# The 9 Centers — Defined vs Undefined

The BodyGraph contains **9 centers** — energy hubs adapted from the chakra system. In Human Design each center is either **defined** (colored in: consistent, reliable, self-generated energy) or **undefined/open** (white: variable energy absorbed from others and the environment).

- **Defined** centers are a person's fixed, dependable nature — they put this energy out into the world consistently.
- **Undefined/open** centers are where a person is flexible, impressionable, and learns about others. They amplify and sample outside energy, which is a source of wisdom over time but also of conditioning (taking on what isn't truly theirs).

## The Nine Centers

| Center | Domain | Type | Defined | Undefined / Open |
|--------|--------|------|---------|------------------|
| **Head (Crown)** | Inspiration, mental pressure, questions | Pressure | Fixed way of being inspired; consistent mental pressure | Absorbs others' questions; can feel pressured to answer thoughts that aren't theirs |
| **Ajna** | Conceptualization, analysis, beliefs | Awareness | Fixed way of thinking and processing; certainty | Mentally flexible, open-minded; may feel pressure to seem certain |
| **Throat** | Communication, manifestation, action | — | Reliable voice/expression; speaks and acts consistently | Speaks variably; can over-talk to get attention; finds its voice through others |
| **G / Identity** | Identity, love, direction | — | Consistent sense of self and direction in life | Searches for identity/direction; chameleon-like; sensitive to place and people |
| **Heart / Ego / Will** | Willpower, ego, value, material world | Motor | Consistent willpower and self-worth; can make/keep promises | Inconsistent willpower; should avoid proving worth or over-promising |
| **Sacral** | Life force, work, sexuality, sustainable energy | Motor | Generator engine; sustainable energy for work and creation | Not a consistent energy source; can overwork to keep up; must rest |
| **Solar Plexus** | Emotions, feelings, moods | Motor / Awareness | Emotional wave; emotional authority; rides own moods | Absorbs and amplifies others' emotions; avoids confrontation; learns emotional wisdom |
| **Spleen** | Intuition, instinct, health, survival, fear | Awareness | Consistent in-the-moment intuition; spontaneous well-being | Holds onto things/relationships out of fear; learns to read health and timing |
| **Root** | Stress, drive, adrenaline, pressure to act | Pressure / Motor | Consistent way of handling pressure and stress | Rushes to relieve outside pressure; learns to act without urgency |

## How Definition Shapes a Person

- The **count and pattern of defined centers** determines **Type** (e.g. a defined Sacral makes a Generator; a motor connected to the Throat without a defined Sacral makes a Manifestor; all centers open makes a Reflector).
- **Three pressure centers** (Head, Root) and **four motors** (Heart, Sacral, Solar Plexus, Root) drive activity; **awareness centers** (Ajna, Spleen, Solar Plexus) provide perception.
- The **highest defined awareness/motor center in the authority hierarchy** sets a person's inner Authority (see authority-guide.md).

## Practical reading

Defined centers tell you what someone reliably *brings* to a team. Open centers tell you where they are *adaptable and absorbent* — strengths when conscious (wisdom, flexibility, empathy) and pitfalls when unconscious (taking on stress, over-talking, over-promising, chasing identity). In SHADDAI, an agent's defined centers indicate its dependable contributions; open centers indicate where it best amplifies or complements its teammates.
