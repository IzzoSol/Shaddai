What is an NFT?

Owning an NFT is like having a one-of-a-kind digital asset. You can choose to keep it as a long-term investment, or simply because you appreciate the art. You can also sell it and potentially make a profit. For creators, NFTs provide the opportunity to receive royalties from the sale of their work. NFTs are being used in a variety of industries, such as gaming, music, real estate, and more. One of the most popular use cases for NFTs is that it provides proof of ownership for digital art, which opens up new possibilities for artists.

What is proof of ownership?

If you own an NFT, the blockchain will track who owns it, and the whole world will know that the NFT belongs to your specific wallet address. This means that no one can dispute your claim of ownership. While anyone can download an image of an Andy Warhol from Google Images, only the owner of a certificate proving ownership of the Andy Warhol can truly own it. This is the power of digital ownership.

How do I setup a Solana Wallet?

1. Download the Phantom wallet from the Phantom website.
2. Install the wallet on your computer or mobile.
3. Open the wallet and create a new account.
4. Enter a secure password and confirm it.
5. You will be given a mnemonic phrase. Make sure to write it down and store it in a safe place.
6. Once you have created your account, you will be asked to enter your mnemonic phrase to confirm your identity.
7. Once you have confirmed your identity, you will be taken to the main wallet page.
8. On the main wallet page, you will be able to view your account balance, send and receive SOL tokens, store and list NFT's and view your transaction history.
9. To receive SOL tokens, you will need to provide your wallet address to the sender.
10. To send SOL tokens, you will need to enter the recipient's wallet address and the amount of SOL tokens you wish to send.
11. Once you have completed the transaction, you will be able to view the transaction in your transaction history.

What are the Fees?

The fees associated with using the Solana Blockchain depend on the type of transaction being performed. Generally, the fees are very low and are designed to incentivize users to use the network.

Buying and selling on marketplaces like Opensea and Magic Eden have small fees associated. Thugnugz royalties are currently set at 0% but is subject to change in the future.

How do I buy $SOL?

To buy SOL, you will need to first set up a wallet that supports SOL. Once you have a wallet, you can purchase SOL from an exchange that supports SOL or buy it directly in from your phantom wallet.

You will need to create an account on the exchange and deposit funds into your account.

Once you have funds in your account, you can search for SOL and place an order to buy it. Once your order is filled, you will have SOL in your wallet.
