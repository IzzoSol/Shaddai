IV.How It Worksthe empire at a glance

Your context passes through the Seven, is refined by the AURA engine, and returns as wrought output — all upon open rails.

x402 — pay-per-call rails MCP — model-context protocol tools BYOK — bring your own free key

Selective tool injection is AURA's newest surface: it sends only the tools a task requires — not the whole armoury — the single greatest saving on every call, yet it fails open, so a tool that might be needed is never withheld. With Distillation (which pares repeated instruction yet spares every rule of safety, of format, and of routing) and history compression, one rite — optimise — works all three at once before a prompt is ever sent, and can fit the whole to a fixed measure of coin.
