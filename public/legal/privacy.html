2. Chat and User-Generated Content

SHADDAI includes multiplayer chat and other user-generated content ("UGC"). Please note:

  • Messages sent in shared/multiplayer spaces are visible to other users in those spaces. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat.
  • We store chat and UGC to operate the Service, enforce our Terms, and moderate abuse.
  • Retention: We keep chat and UGC only as long as needed to run and moderate the Service, or as required by law. You can request deletion of your content (see "Your Rights"). Some records may be retained longer where necessary for safety, legal, or fraud-prevention reasons.
  • Prompts you submit may be processed by AI model providers to generate output. Do not put confidential data into prompts.

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us run SHADDAI, and only as needed:

  • Legal — we may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
