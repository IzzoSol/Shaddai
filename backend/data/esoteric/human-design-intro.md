# Human Design — Introduction

Human Design is a synthesis system created by Ra Uru Hu in 1987. It combines five traditions — the I Ching, the Hindu-Brahmin chakra system, the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, Western astrology, and modern physics (quantum theory and neutrino science) — into a single map called the **BodyGraph**.

The premise: each person has a consistent way of making decisions and engaging with the world that, when followed, reduces resistance ("deconditioning") and aligns them with their authentic nature. The system is descriptive and experiential rather than predictive — it is used as a framework for self-understanding, not fortune-telling.

## The BodyGraph

A chart is calculated from birth date, time, and location. It produces a diagram of **9 centers** (energy hubs), **64 gates** (drawn from the I Ching hexagrams), and **36 channels** connecting the centers. Centers and channels are either **defined** (colored in, consistent energy) or **undefined/open** (white, variable energy taken in from the environment).

Two key calculation points feed the chart: the **Personality** (conscious mind, your birth moment) and the **Design** (unconscious body, roughly 88 days before birth). Both are layered onto the same BodyGraph.

## The Five Types

Type is the most foundational element. It is determined by which centers are defined and how they connect, and it governs each person's **Strategy** for engaging with life.

| Type | % of population (approx.) | Core nature |
|------|---------------------------|-------------|
| **Manifestor** | ~9% | Initiators. Designed to start things and act independently. |
| **Generator** | ~37% | Builders. Sustainable life-force energy for work they love. |
| **Manifesting Generator** | ~33% | Multi-passionate builders who also initiate; fast and non-linear. |
| **Projector** | ~20% | Guides. See and direct others' energy; need recognition. |
| **Reflector** | ~1% | Mirrors. Fully open; sample and reflect the health of their community. |

Generators and Manifesting Generators together (~70%) are the energy types that respond and build; Manifestors initiate; Projectors guide; Reflectors evaluate.

## How to Use a Chart

A practical reading focuses on four anchors:

1. **Type** — your overall energy mechanic and broad role.
2. **Strategy** — the correct way to engage (e.g. respond, wait for invitation, inform before acting).
3. **Authority** — your reliable inner decision-making process (see authority-guide.md).
4. **Profile** — your "costume" or personality theme, expressed as two numbers (see profile-roles.md).

Together these are often summarized as a one-line signature, e.g. *"Generator 2/4, Sacral Authority — respond, then trust the gut yes/no."*

In the SHADDAI system, each agent carries such a signature. This corpus is the reference library agents consult when a task calls for esoteric or interpretive reasoning, so they ground their output in documented Human Design material rather than inventing doctrine.

## Caveat

Human Design is an esoteric, experiential framework. It is not science and makes no medical or psychological claims. It is used here as a structured, well-documented language for describing working styles and collaboration patterns.
