## THESIS
Building capacity is crucial for achieving success, especially when faced with uncertainty, and it involves developing skills, saving money, and creating opportunities to capitalize on potential advantages. By focusing on capacity building, individuals can prepare themselves to recognize and seize opportunities as they arise. This approach enables people to take control of their lives and increase their chances of success.

## FRAMEWORKS & PRINCIPLES
- Develop skills to increase earning capacity
- Save money to provide resources for opportunities
- Manage time effectively to maximize optionality
- Build an audience to create leverage
- Expand network to increase "luck surface area"

## CHAPTERS
### Introduction to Building Capacity
- Key teaching: To achieve success, especially when unsure of what to do, one should focus on building capacity, which includes developing skills, saving money, and creating opportunities.
- Quote: "Don't be in the bleachers when the fat pitch comes."

### Building Capacity through Preparation
- Key teaching: Building capacity involves preparing oneself through actions like going to bed on time, getting in shape, and saving money, which enables one to recognize and capitalize on opportunities.
- Quote: "You want to be at the plate, you want to have already practiced your swing, and you want to be ready to go."

### The Importance of Time and Money in Building Capacity
- Key teaching: Saving money and managing time effectively are crucial in building capacity, as they provide the resources needed to take advantage of opportunities.
- Quote: "Money buys time and time buys optionality."

### Acquiring Skills to Increase Earning Capacity
- Key teaching: Acquiring new skills is essential for increasing one's earning capacity, and skills are inflation-proof, making them a valuable investment.
- Quote: "Skills are inflation-proof."

### Building an Audience without a Product
- Key teaching: Building an audience without a product can provide leverage and potential energy, allowing one to launch a successful product or service when the time is right.
- Quote: "Attention gives you leverage."

### Building a Network to Expand Opportunities
- Key teaching: Building a network by meeting new people and expanding one's "luck surface area" can lead to new opportunities and increase the chances of success.
- Quote: "The fastest way to change your life is to change the people who are around you who affect your life."
