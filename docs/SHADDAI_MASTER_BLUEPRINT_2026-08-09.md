## THE ROOM SYSTEM — INN, HOME & PRO ROOMS

### The Rule
```

                Cannot customize. Cannot own.

PRO USERS:      Can OWN a room (in their office OR at an Inn slot)
                Can decorate, set rent price, collect from tenants
                Can place custom furniture via the Forge
```
